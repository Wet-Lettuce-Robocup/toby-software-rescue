### Reflection
I had issues with calculating the distance and angle to the ball, where if the ball was roughly 45º from the centre of the camera view, it would be detected as 70º away. This was due to distortion in the camera lens which can be corrected by calibrating the camera. cv2 has a built in camera calibration tool, which I used to undistort camera frames, leading to more accurate distance and angle calculations.

### Decisions made
I decided to attempt to fix the camera distortion using cv2's calibration tools. This involved taking multiple photos of a chessboard, as it used the squares to calculate the necessary distortion coefficients (radial distortion and some tangential distortion) to counter the effects of this distortion when calculating distances and angles to objects.

### Testing notes
Balls were detected at roughly the correct distance (usually with an error of ±30mm), however the angle to the ball was extremely inaccurate at large angles (40º+) due to camera distortion.
Everything seems to be working well with the 1-class model, so I feel confident to expand it to 2 classes.
